What we mean by VPE

Viable Provision Emergence

Big name. Simple goal: your satisfaction.

Viable

For your table

It has to be real and right. Real food from a seller who checks out, lawful where you live, lawful when it crosses state lines, and a price and a trip that make sense. If it doesn’t add up, we don’t bring it.

For your business

Your name, your product, your way. We confirm what the law requires for each place you sell, so every order you take is one you can stand behind.

Provisions

For your table

Everything that feeds you: fresh catch, meats, produce, groceries, a simple prepared meal from a kitchen near you, at your door or ready for pickup. And the specialties you can’t find where you live:

  • Authentic seasonings and spice blends, the real Cajun and Creole kind
  • Small-batch hot sauces, dark roux, filé and cane syrup
  • Coffee and chicory, pralines, mayhaw and fig preserves
  • Louisiana rice, pecans and local honey
  • Boudin, andouille and smoked meats from inspected kitchens

Each comes within what the law allows: some travel anywhere, and some stay close to home.

For your business

If you grow it, catch it, cook it, stock it, pack it or deliver it, there’s a place for you. A shrimper’s catch, a grocer’s shelf, a restaurant’s plate, a smokehouse’s sausage: SmartPalate brings you customers who are already asking.

Emergence

For your table

We take your request and bring it together, from everything around you or from anywhere in the US. The more people answer “What’cha got a taste for!”, the more SmartPalate focuses on getting it done, closer to home.

For your business

Demand comes to you, counted and organized. Many households and kitchens can add up to one order that makes the trip worth it, and you stay the seller.

Your own personal food network, pieced together for you. That’s VPE.
