What we mean by VPE

Viable Provision Emergence

It's a big name for a simple idea: you tell us what you want, and we bring together the people who can get it to you.

1

You tell us what you've got a taste for

Gulf shrimp, fresh crawfish, a cut of meat you can't find, or something from back home.

2

We find who has it

A grower, a grocer, a restaurant or a fisherman, even in another state.

3

We get it to you

Through a delivery partner or a local pickup.

Viable

It has to make sense: the food is real, the seller is legit, and the price and the trip work.

Provision

Food and supplies.

Emergence

The supply grows out of what people actually ask for, pulled together from the fragmented food industry all around you.

The more people ask for something, the sooner we can bring it.
